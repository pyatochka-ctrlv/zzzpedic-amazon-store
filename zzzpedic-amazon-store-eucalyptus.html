Our story

Built around one idea: temperature is the missing piece of sleep.

ZzzPedic started with a simple frustration — waking up overheated, night after night. So the team set out to engineer bedding around thermoregulation first, comfort second, using dual-sided cooling fabrics and bamboo-derived viscose instead of the synthetic blends most "cooling" bedding relies on.

Sleep specialists note that a consistently cool sleep surface is one of the more reliable ways to prevent the overheating that disrupts deep sleep. Laura McKeenan, M.D. — Somnologist
